Global Flavors From Our Journey

Gathered from homestays, community kitchens, and student-led cooking nights, these comforting dishes celebrate the people and places we explore together.

Comfort Kitchen Collection

Save these easy-to-follow recipes for your next cultural night or service learning fundraiser. Each one was shared with us on the road.

🥗 Olivier Salad

A festive staple packed with potatoes, carrots, eggs, and smoked sausage.

  • Boil potatoes, carrots, and eggs until tender.
  • Dice vegetables, sausage, and pickles into small cubes.
  • Fold in peas and mayonnaise, season, and chill before serving.

🍝 Pasta Bolognese

Italian pasta layered with a rich tomato and herb meat sauce.

  • Sauté minced meat with onion, garlic, and herbs.
  • Add crushed tomatoes and simmer for 20 minutes.
  • Toss al dente pasta with the sauce and finish with parmesan.

🍰 Chocolate Cake

A fluffy dessert with deep cocoa notes and glossy glaze.

  • Whisk flour, cocoa, sugar, and eggs until smooth.
  • Blend in milk and vegetable oil for a silky batter.
  • Bake 40 minutes at 180°C, then crown with chocolate glaze.

🍲 Borscht

Traditional beetroot soup served hot with sour cream and herbs.

  • Simmer beef or chicken with aromatics for a rich broth.
  • Add potatoes and shredded cabbage to the stock.
  • Finish with sautéed beets, carrots, onions, and season to taste.

🍕 Margherita Pizza

A wood-fired favorite topped with tomatoes, mozzarella, and basil.

  • Stretch dough and swipe on a bright tomato sauce.
  • Layer with fresh mozzarella and basil leaves.
  • Bake at high heat for 12–15 minutes until golden.

🍛 Plov

Fragrant Uzbek pilaf scented with cumin, coriander, and tender lamb.

  • Sear lamb with onions and julienned carrots.
  • Add rinsed rice, whole garlic, and warm water or stock.
  • Steam gently under a lid until grains are fluffy.

🥞 Pancakes with Honey

Delicate crepes perfect for breakfast clubs or pop-up cafés.

  • Blend milk, eggs, flour, sugar, and a pinch of salt.
  • Cook thin pancakes on a hot buttered skillet.
  • Serve stacked with honey or your favourite jam.

🥗 Caesar Salad with Chicken

A crunchy classic finished with tangy dressing and parmesan snow.

  • Sear seasoned chicken breasts until golden and slice.
  • Tear crisp lettuce, add croutons and shaved parmesan.
  • Toss with Caesar dressing and top with the warm chicken.

🍣 Sushi Rolls

Japanese delicacy combining vinegared rice, seaweed, and fresh fish or vegetables.

  • Rinse rice until water runs clear and cook with a splash of rice vinegar.
  • Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo mat, spread rice evenly over it.
  • Add fillings like salmon, cucumber, or avocado and roll tightly.
  • Slice into bite-sized pieces and serve with soy sauce and wasabi.
