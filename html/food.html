🥗 Olivier Salad

A classic salad with potatoes, carrots, eggs, and sausage.

  • Boil potatoes, carrots, and eggs.
  • Dice vegetables, sausage, and pickles.
  • Add peas and mayonnaise, then mix well.

🍝 Pasta Bolognese

Italian pasta with aromatic meat sauce.

  • Fry minced meat with onion and garlic.
  • Add tomato sauce and simmer for 20 minutes.
  • Cook pasta and mix with the sauce.

🍰 Chocolate Cake

A tender cake with rich chocolate flavor.

  • Mix flour, cocoa, sugar, and eggs.
  • Add milk and vegetable oil.
  • Bake for 40 minutes at 180°C, decorate with glaze.

🍲 Borscht

Traditional Ukrainian soup with cabbage, beets, and meat.

  • Boil meat, add chopped potatoes.
  • Fry beets, carrots, and onions, add to the soup.
  • Salt, season, and serve with sour cream.

🍕 Margherita Pizza

Classic Italian pizza with tomatoes, cheese, and basil.

  • Roll out dough and spread tomato sauce.
  • Add mozzarella cheese and basil leaves.
  • Bake for 15 minutes at 220°C.

🍛 Plov

Fragrant Uzbek pilaf with carrots, rice, and lamb.

  • Fry meat with onions and carrots.
  • Add rice and spices, pour in water.
  • Simmer covered until rice is cooked.

🥞 Pancakes with Honey

Thin homemade pancakes served with honey or jam.

  • Mix milk, eggs, flour, and sugar.
  • Fry pancakes on both sides in a pan.
  • Serve with honey or jam.

🥗 Caesar Salad with Chicken

Popular salad with chicken, croutons, and Caesar dressing.

  • Fry chicken until golden brown.
  • Tear lettuce leaves and add croutons.
  • Mix with Caesar dressing and sprinkle with parmesan.
