About Dunya School

Dunya School is a modern international learning community in Baku, Azerbaijan. We are proud to be part of a school that encourages creativity, global awareness, and sustainable thinking. Through projects like Dunya Horizons, we explore real-world challenges, develop critical thinking, and connect classroom learning with meaningful global action.
