Our Mission

To inspire young minds to think globally, act sustainably, and learn through exploration. Dunya Horizons is not just a project — it’s a vision of the future shaped by students.

About Dunya School

Dunya School in Baku is more than a place to study — it’s a space for innovation, creativity, and global connection. We learn to think beyond textbooks and use our knowledge to make a real difference in the world.

Our Project: Dunya Horizons

This initiative was born from students’ curiosity and drive to solve real-world challenges. Through Dunya Horizons, we explore environmental issues like the drying of the Caspian Sea, use technology such as satellites and data analysis, and design creative solutions for sustainability. Our goal is to bridge science, action, and awareness — one idea at a time.
